Privacy Policy for n8n-st

Effective Date: August 13, 2025 Last Updated: August 13, 2025 App: n8n-st (Facebook Integration)
01

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how n8n-st ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information obtained through its integration with the Meta (Facebook) platform. By using this application, you agree to the practices described below.

This App is used to automate Facebook Page posting via n8n workflows and does not collect or store personal data beyond what is necessary for the automation to function.

02

Information We Collect

When you connect this App to your Facebook account, the following information may be accessed:

  • Your Facebook public profile (name, profile ID)
  • The list of Facebook Pages you manage
  • Page access tokens used to post on your behalf
  • Post content (text, images, links) that you configure in your automation workflows
  • Basic engagement metadata on posts created by the App
03

How We Use Your Information

The information accessed is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Authenticating your identity and authorizing access to Facebook Pages
  • Publishing posts to your Facebook Pages as configured in your n8n workflows
  • Reading post performance data when requested by your workflows
  • Managing and responding to comments or interactions if enabled in your workflow

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than executing the automation you configured.

04

Facebook Permissions Used

This App requests the following Meta permissions to operate:

  • pages_show_list — To display and select the Pages you manage
  • pages_manage_posts — To create, edit, and delete posts on your Pages
  • pages_read_engagement — To read post and page engagement data
  • pages_manage_engagement — To manage comments and reactions on posts
  • public_profile — Basic profile info required by Facebook Login
05

Data Storage & Retention

This App operates primarily as a pass-through automation layer via n8n. We do not maintain a central database of your personal information.

  • Access tokens are stored only within your own n8n instance and are not shared externally
  • Workflow logs may temporarily store post content for debugging purposes
  • No personal data is retained after you disconnect the integration
06

Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data:

  • All API communications use HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Access tokens are stored securely within your n8n credential store
  • No sensitive credentials are logged or exposed in workflow outputs
07

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Revoke the App's access to your Facebook account at any time via Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites
  • Request deletion of any data associated with your account
  • Disconnect the integration from within your n8n instance at any time
08

Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, revoke App access from Facebook Settings → Security and Login → Apps and Websites, or contact us at the email below. All associated tokens and workflow configurations will be invalidated immediately upon revocation.

09

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
